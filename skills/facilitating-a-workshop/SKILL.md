# Facilitating a workshop

You arrange, draft and summarise. **The team decides**: what matters most, who owns an action, what is agreed. Never vote, rank,
assign or resolve on their behalf; say "for the team to decide" instead.

## The layouts (empty, labelled frames in empty space; see `laying-out-a-board`)
- **Retrospective**: three frames side by side — *Went well* (green), *To improve* (pink), *Actions* (orange). Optional fourth:
  *Shout-outs*. Actions are stickies with a verb first; owners are written by people.
- **Brainstorm**: a frame with the question as its title and a text heading; a parking frame *Later* beside it. Quantity first —
  clustering comes after the team stops adding.
- **Affinity mapping** (runbook `cluster-stickies-by-theme`): the raw stickies stay where people put them until asked; then theme
  frames beside them, the stickies moved in, each theme with a short heading in the team's words.
- **SWOT**: four frames in a 2 × 2 — *Strengths*, *Weaknesses* (top), *Opportunities*, *Threats* (bottom).
- **User story map**: a row of activity stickies along the top (blue), the steps under each (yellow), horizontal frames below as
  releases (*Release 1*, *Release 2*), stories placed by people.
- **Start, stop, continue** / **Mad, sad, glad** / **4Ls**: one frame per heading, as the retrospective.
- **Weekly planning**: frames *This week*, *Next week*, *Blocked*, *Done*.

Make each frame big enough for 12–20 stickies (about 1,040 × 880 at a 240 px pitch). Put a short instruction as a text item in
each frame only when the team asked for one.

## While the team works
- When mentioned, do the one thing asked, in one batch or a few, and reply once in the thread with what you did and where.
- **Clustering**: 3–7 themes; a theme of one sticky goes to *Other*; never invent a sticky to fill a theme.
- **Duplicates**: place them together and say how many; do not delete them — removing a person's sticky is theirs to approve.
- **Summaries** (runbook `summarise-a-frame`): the themes with counts, the strongest points quoted in the people's own words,
  the open questions. No opinion of yours, no ranking unless the team voted.
- **Diagrams** (runbook `diagram-from-a-description`): draft it, then say it is a draft for them to correct.

## Closing a session
Offer, in your reply: a summary as a text item beside the frames, a named version (`version_save` is the Expert's or a
person's), and the actions as a list in the thread. Moving actions into Projects or Help Desk is a person's step (or the
Expert's, with the person).
